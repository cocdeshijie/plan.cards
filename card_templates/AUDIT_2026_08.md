# Card template audit — 2026-08-06

Four rounds of multi-source research (~430 agents, 3-vote adversarial verification per claim)
against the 403 current templates. Every item below was reviewed and approved individually.

Sources are recorded per finding in the research output; the highest-grade evidence classes used
were issuer first-person notices (Citi Custom Cash, TD/RH), regulatory filings (FDIC BankFind for
Discover Bank and the Comenity charters; Bread Financial and Synchrony 10-Ks), and issuer press
releases carrying regulated pricing disclosures (Citi AAdvantage Executive).

## 1. Schema change (prerequisite for the discontinuation items)

- Add a first-class status field: `status: active | closed_to_new_applicants | discontinued`
  plus `status_date`. Existing cardholders must keep tracking a dead card; only NEW card
  creation should be blocked. Requires model + migration + template loader + frontend work.
- Deferred, noted but not approved: multi-year credit cycles (the 4-year Global Entry /
  TSA PreCheck credits on both BoA templates, and HSBC Elite's 4.5-year airport-screening
  credit, are correct facts stranded in `notes:` where reset logic cannot see them).

spark_cash -> Spark 2% Cash, spark_cash_plus -> Spark 2% Cash Plus,
spark_cash_select -> Spark 1.5% Cash Select, spark_classic -> Spark 1% Classic.

**Correction made during implementation.** The research said to rename `spark_miles` ->
"Venture Business" and `spark_miles_select` -> "VentureOne Business". Doing so produced
DUPLICATES: `capitalone/venture_biz` and `capitalone/ventureone_biz` already existed with
exactly those names and matching fees ($95 / $0). The library already carried the renamed
products; the spark_miles pair are the pre-rename templates for the same cards. They were
therefore marked `status: discontinued` with a note pointing at the successor, not renamed.
`test_template_names_are_unique` was added to make this class of mistake fail CI.
