# Forum card-art additions: legacy products and Flying Blue

Reviewed 2026-09-09. Eleven approved product identities. Version IDs record review year, not product launch. Legacy fees are explicitly historical where the current issuer servicing page omits pricing; current account terms prevail. Exact closure dates are omitted when evidence only establishes an approximate period.

## boa/flying_blue

Earns Flying Blue miles. Current Visa Signature terms: $89 annual fee, 3x on Air France/KLM/eligible SkyTeam and dining, 1.5x elsewhere. Includes 20 anniversary XP; spend $15,000 in the anniversary year for 80 additional XP, and $25,000 for another 60 XP (160 total). Earn 5,000 anniversary miles after $50 in anniversary-year purchases. No foreign transaction fees. Earlier World Elite Mastercard artwork is retained as a historical cover; those historical accounts may have different reward terms.

- [Issuer, partner, or issuer-distributed source](https://www.bankofamerica.com/credit-cards/)
- [Issuer, partner, or issuer-distributed source](https://www.bankofamerica.com/credit-cards/products/air-france-credit-card/)

## boa/better_balance_rewards

Discontinued historical product with a $0 annual fee. Accounts were converted to Unlimited Cash Rewards in May 2023. The former payment-based program offered $25 quarterly, plus $5 with a qualifying Bank of America checking or savings account; payment eligibility conditions applied. These ended rewards are described for account history and are not ongoing credits.

- [Issuer, partner, or issuer-distributed source](https://www.bankofamerica.com/betterbalancerewards)

## boa/merrill_plus

Legacy card closed to new applications with a historically documented $0 annual fee. The issuer’s 2022 guide describes 1 Merrill Point per dollar and eligible air rewards starting at 25,000 points for a ticket up to $500 booked through the program. Plus Level requires $50,000 in calendar-year purchases; its up-to-$200 airline incidental credit requires enrollment and is conditional. Existing account terms control continued pricing and benefits.

## barclays/arrival

Legacy no-annual-fee Arrival card, closed to new applicants. Distinct from Arrival Plus and Arrival Premier. Historical program earns 2 miles per dollar on dining and travel and 1 mile elsewhere; existing account terms govern continued rewards. The issuer’s historical press release identifies this product separately from Arrival Plus.

## barclays/rewards

Legacy U.S. Barclaycard Rewards Mastercard, closed to new applicants. Historical program earned 2 points per dollar on gas, groceries and utilities and 1 point elsewhere. Not the UK Barclaycard Rewards Visa. Current account-specific reward terms should be checked with Barclays; the archived U.S. marketing page is no longer available.

Original Freedom Visa, closed to new applications; existing cardmembers continue to earn Ultimate Rewards. Quarterly bonus categories require activation and apply to up to $1,500 in combined category purchases each quarter. This product is distinct from Freedom Flex and Freedom Unlimited.

## chase/ihg_select

Closed to new cardmembers. Historically documented annual fee is $49. Includes IHG Platinum Elite status and an anniversary Free Night with a 40,000-point redemption cap. Historical program also provides a 10% points-redemption rebate, up to 100,000 rebated points per calendar year; consult the account benefit terms. No foreign transaction fees. Historical IHG Rewards Club Select covers identify the same product.

## chase/ritz_carlton

Closed to new applications. Includes an anniversary Free Night Award up to 85,000 points, Marriott Bonvoy Gold Elite status and 15 annual elite night credits. Airline incidental credit requires contacting Chase within four billing cycles; airfare is excluded. Includes three Club Level upgrade certificates for qualifying paid stays per account anniversary year. Qualifying two-night hotel-stay credits are per stay, not annual statement credits.

## citi/att_access_more

Legacy card closed to new applications. Earns ThankYou Points, distinct from AT&T Access and AT&T Points Plus. Historical annual fee is $95. Eligible online retail/travel purchases and direct AT&T purchases earn 3x; exclusions apply, including rent, taxes, utilities and other ineligible merchant categories. The annual 10,000-point bonus requires $10,000 in membership-year purchases. Existing account terms govern pricing and eligibility.

## citi/dividend

Legacy cash-back card closed to new applicants; historical annual fee is $0. Quarterly 5% categories require activation. Standard purchase cash back is limited to $300 per calendar year, including both 5% category earnings and 1% other purchases. This is a cash-back earnings limit, not a spending cap. Qualifying Bonus Cash Center bonus earnings are excluded from that limit. Current categories and account-specific redemption terms should be checked with Citi.

- [Issuer, partner, or issuer-distributed source](https://www.citigroup.com/global/news/perspectives/2013/you-spoke-we-listened-1)

## Evidence limits

Flying Blue’s exact product page was reached by following Bank of America’s product-list link. It confirms the Visa Signature title but reports temporarily unavailable; the live issuer card list supplies the detailed $89, 3x/3x/1.5x and anniversary terms. Mastercard covers are historical, not evidence that the current Visa terms applied to all historical accounts.

AT&T fee and 1x baseline are corroborated by Doctor of Credit’s April 2015 launch review. Citi’s indexed January 2024 Access More terms corroborate the membership-year 10,000-point bonus after $10,000 spend; direct retrieval currently redirects to an error page. The bonus is modeled as a spend threshold, not a statement credit.

Merrill’s issuer 2022 guide supplies earnings and conditional Plus Level benefits; contemporaneous 2017 reporting supplies the $0 fee and closure to new applications. IHG Select’s $49 fee comes from the 2016 offer report; current Chase servicing confirms the legacy product and capped free night. [Frequent Miler’s historical explanation](https://frequentmiler.com/ihg-sweet-spot-dynamic-pricing-4th-night-free/) clarifies that the annual limit is 100,000 rebated points (up to one million points redeemed), not 100,000 points redeemed.

Dividend’s issuer history confirms the product and rotating rewards; NerdWallet’s July 2026 review supplies $0 fee, 1% baseline and $300 combined calendar-year cash-back cap. The schema only supports a dollar spending cap, so the cash-back limit is preserved in notes.

Better Balance’s old issuer URL no longer serves the program terms. The 2023 contemporary report reproduces an issuer conversion-letter image and documents $0 fee and May 2023 conversion. Ended rewards are descriptive only. Barclays Rewards uses a clearly labeled NerdWallet historical review because the original U.S. marketing page is unavailable. ThankYou Preferred and AT&T correctly carry points/ThankYou tags, not cashback.
