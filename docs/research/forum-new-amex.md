## Limits and decisions

- The EveryDay acquisition pages no longer expose an application body, while issuer pages retain existing-member benefits. Closure is also corroborated by a [report quoting American Express's September 2024 confirmation](https://www.nerdwallet.com/credit-cards/reviews/amex-everyday) and [contemporaneous coverage of both products](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/amex-everyday-amex-everyday-preferred-no-longer-accepting-new-applications/). No exact status date was invented. Fees are supported by issuer material, but the Preferred agreement is historical.
- Centurion's latest agreement verified in this review is dated June 2025. Its fee is sourced, but its complete current personal-benefit package was not publicly verified. Corporate/Business Centurion terms were deliberately not copied. The template identifies the limitation instead of inventing credits or invitation-spend requirements.
- Schwab's application disclosure is self-dated September 8, 2026. Conditional holdings bonuses, the December Uber increment, fractional-dollar Walmart+ reimbursement, and four-year application-fee reimbursement remain notes because the current schema cannot represent them faithfully. Current application terms do not list Saks; no Saks credit was copied from the generic Platinum template. CLEAR+ is $219 in the reviewed current source.
- The old Morgan Stanley anniversary award appears in historical material but was not found in current benefit terms, so it is not automatically populated. Zero annual fee is supported by the issuer agreement, not assumed.
- Neither EveryDay card is a Blue Cash card. Schwab Investor is cash back, while Morgan Stanley Credit is Membership Rewards. Generic Platinum, supplementary Companion Platinum, and Employee Business Expense remain separate concepts.

Validation: direct Pydantic schema validation passed for all six new files. Full repository validation is assigned to root.
